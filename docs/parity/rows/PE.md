# Parity rows, PE: Contractor portal and eCommerce

The detail behind the [PE rows of the parity matrix](../LBM-PARITY-MATRIX.md#pe-contractor-portal-and-ecommerce). R, B and A refs link the Rundoo, BisTrack and Agility pages each claim rests on; Gable's evidence links its code on `refactor/v1` at `58dde7e8`.

## PE-01

**Contractor portal (account, invoices, statements, documents).** Priority **MUST**: The contractor portal is named in the goal.

- **Rundoo** (Yes, documented): Free customer app: balance in aging buckets, open invoices, statements, purchase history and payment methods; sign in by phone number and texted code; statements carry a QR code to the app. Multi yard: A contractor set up as separate customers at two locations picks the account after sign in. Add on or partner: Free app for customers; included in the subscription. [R77](https://www.rundocs.com/help/getting-started/mobile-apps/) [R10](https://rundoo.ai/product/customer-self-service-app/) [R4](https://rundoo.ai/pricing/)
- **BisTrack** (Yes, marketing only): Web Track portal gives account customers copy invoices, statements, proof of delivery, order and job information, and catalogs at their negotiated prices, with 24x7 access; Epicor Identity covers customer portal sign in. Add on or partner: Epicor Web Track. [B37](https://www.epicor.com/en-uk/resource-center/success-stories/jw-grant) [B68](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/resources/success-stories/building-supply/alliance-lumber/) [B39](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/resources/success-stories/building-supply/chinook-lumber/) [B66](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/resources/success-stories/building-supply/hamlin-building/) [B33](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/blog/technology-and-data/introducing-bistrack-web/)
- **Agility** (Yes, documented): Agility Commerce Cloud (and the older PartnerView) give customers their pricing, stock, orders, quotes, credit memos and invoices; the Billing Portal adds statements, payment history and documents. Add on or partner: Agility Commerce Cloud (optional product); Agility Billing Portal. [A138](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Integrated_Solutions/agilitycommercecloud.htm) [A139](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Integrated_Solutions/partnerview.htm) [A10](https://www.dmsi.com/agility-billing-portal/) [A120](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/System_Administration/securitydataallocation.htm)
- **Gable today** (Partial): Contractor portal with its own login (httpOnly cookie): dashboard with balance and credit, orders, invoices, deliveries, projects, team users, invites and roles; every read scoped to the session's customer; desk portal pages. **Missing:** No statements or aging in the portal; the invoice download is a text file, not the PDF; money is float dollars; no document library (proof of delivery file, certificates, quote PDFs); not on the module recipe. **The MVP:** backend/internal/portal: the same login, dashboard, orders, invoices, deliveries, catalog, cart and team routes, without quotes or reschedule Evidence: [`core/internal/portal/handler.go:64`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/portal/handler.go#L64), [`core/internal/portal/handler.go:75`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/portal/handler.go#L75), [`core/internal/portal/handler.go:81`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/portal/handler.go#L81), [`core/internal/portal/handler.go:113`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/portal/handler.go#L113), [`core/internal/portal/tenancy_pg_test.go:172`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/portal/tenancy_pg_test.go#L172), [`web/apps/desk/src/pages/portal/PortalInvoices.ts:78`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/web/apps/desk/src/pages/portal/PortalInvoices.ts#L78). ## PE-02

**Online bill pay.** Priority **MUST**: All three take portal payments; the gateway account is Colton's, so the build is the seam and a test gateway.

- **Rundoo** (Yes, documented): Customers pay statement, total balance or chosen invoices by card or ACH in the Customer App; autopay of statement balances from a saved card or bank; bank link sent by email and text. Add on or partner: Included at no extra charge (vendor claim). [R10](https://rundoo.ai/product/customer-self-service-app/) [R35](https://www.rundocs.com/help/customers-jobs/customers/) [R37](https://www.rundocs.com/help/customers-jobs/statements/)
- **BisTrack** (Yes, marketing only): Customers pay their accounts through the Web Track portal without giving card details by phone; a dealer chose BisTrack for 24x7 account access and payment. Add on or partner: Epicor Web Track. [B37](https://www.epicor.com/en-uk/resource-center/success-stories/jw-grant) [B66](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/resources/success-stories/building-supply/hamlin-building/)
- **Agility** (Yes, documented): Customers view and pay invoices online by card or ACH and can apply credit memos; payment methods cover eCommerce and ACH payments. Add on or partner: Agility Billing Portal or Commerce Cloud; Billtrust (partner). [A138](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Integrated_Solutions/agilitycommercecloud.htm) [A10](https://www.dmsi.com/agility-billing-portal/) [A41](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Data_Files/paymentmethodmaintenance.htm) [A27](https://restdoc.dmsi.com/)
- **Gable today** (Missing): No portal payment route or screen. The ERP has a Run Payments keyed card flow (intent, then charge) that records and applies a payment, with no desk caller. **Missing:** Portal pay of one or many invoices by card or ACH, saved methods, convenience fees, receipts, and posting through the AR core with the portal user as actor.
