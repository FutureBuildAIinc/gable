# Parity rows, AR: AR, credit, statements, finance charges, liens and job accounts

The detail behind the [AR rows of the parity matrix](../LBM-PARITY-MATRIX.md#ar-ar-credit-statements-finance-charges-liens-and-job-accounts). R, B and A refs link the Rundoo, BisTrack and Agility pages each claim rests on; Gable's evidence links its code on `refactor/v1` at `58dde7e8`.

## AR-01

**Customer accounts, credit limits and terms.** Priority **MUST**: Accounts, limits and terms shared by every yard.

- **Rundoo** (Yes, documented): Finance term makes a customer a charge account (statement or invoice based); credit limit caps the balance and blocks the charge tender; finance charge assigned per customer; aging buckets on the account. Multi yard: AR control account mapped per location; one customer ledger across stores, so an account charged at one yard can be paid at the other. [R35](https://www.rundocs.com/help/customers-jobs/customers/) [R38](https://www.rundocs.com/help/accounts-receivable/finance-terms/) [R2](https://rundoo.ai/industries/lumber/) [R110](https://rundoo.substack.com/p/rundoo-primer-a-client-story-vetter)
- **BisTrack** (Yes, documented): Credit control and automated credit and collections are core areas; credit limits sit on the customer's Accounts tab, with holds released by credit controller codes; payment terms are set per customer; customer records hold job sites and lien information. [B60](https://www.g2.com/products/epicor-bistrack/reviews) [B20](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/solutions/industries/building-supply/pro-dealers/) [B50](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/solutions/industries/building-supply/epicor-bistrack-for-distributors/) [B8](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/products/enterprise-resource-planning-erp/bistrack/bistrack-api/) [B58](https://www.dckap.com/blog/epicor-bistrack-ecommerce-integration/) [B80](https://ondemand.epicor.com/courseResources/r2049_BT122CreditControlsOverviewTranscript_1.pdf) [B74](https://ondemand.epicor.com/courseResources/r2046_CustomerAccountsOverviewTranscript_1.pdf)
- **Agility** (Yes, documented): Sold-to, bill-to and ship-to accounts with credit limits and expiry, bureau ratings, credit manager and terms; credit checks at chosen levels; home branch for credit. Multi yard: Credit groups limit exposure to branches that share customers. [A27](https://restdoc.dmsi.com/) [A90](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Data_Files/creditgroups.htm) [A91](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Data_Files/homebranchforcustomer.htm)
- **Gable today** (Partial): Customer master on the recipe: credit limit (null none, zero no credit), payment terms by id, PO required, currency, ship-tos with tax rate, contacts with order authority; account summary with available credit; branch-held customers with shared-customer field rules; desk forms. **Missing:** No route shares a customer with another branch (customer_branches written only at create), so other yards' branch-scoped users cannot see it; no credit application or review fields; tier and price level pre ADR 0006. **Open work:** PR 119: a customer create takes its primary branch from the caller's grants; the account surface holds the customer wall. **The MVP:** backend/internal/customer: free text terms and a zero limit meaning no limit. Evidence: [`core/internal/customer/handler.go:66`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/customer/handler.go#L66), [`core/internal/customer/model.go:80`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/customer/model.go#L80), [`core/internal/customer/shared.go:16`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/customer/shared.go#L16), [`core/internal/customer/repository.go:155`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/customer/repository.go#L155), [`core/internal/customer/repository.go:266`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/customer/repository.go#L266), [`core/internal/account/handler.go:39`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/account/handler.go#L39), [`core/internal/customer/wire_test.go:679`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/customer/wire_test.go#L679), [`web/apps/desk/src/pages/accounts/CustomerForm.ts:90`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/web/apps/desk/src/pages/accounts/CustomerForm.ts#L90). ## AR-02

**Credit holds and the credit approval workflow.** Priority **MUST**: Credit holds and approvals are core credit control.

- **Rundoo** (Partial, documented): Credit hold flag and credit limit block the charge tender at the register; no approval queue; Dooey routines can flag accounts but cannot hold a sale for sign off. [R84](https://www.rundocs.com/help/customers-jobs/customer-imports/) [R35](https://www.rundocs.com/help/customers-jobs/customers/) [R75](https://www.rundocs.com/help/rundoo-ai/ai-agents/)
- **BisTrack** (Yes, marketing only): Credit status changes cascade so held customers' orders stop at once; a dealer cut credit code releases by 80 percent with a BisTrack fed report and a new release process. [B26](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/resources/success-stories/building-supply/eikenhout-inc/)
- **Agility** (Yes, documented): Orders failing credit checks go on a system credit hold; the credit approval screen approves, denies or cancels; an order stays held until its check level changes, it is reduced, or a higher limit or a payment clears it. Multi yard: Credit holds update across branches that share customers or a credit branch group. Add on or partner: Nuvo (credit partner). [A108](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Accounts_Receivable/a.rcreditapproval.htm) [A70](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Procedures_Processes/creditholdsalesorders.htm) [A27](https://restdoc.dmsi.com/) [A15](https://www.dmsi.com/partners/)
- **Gable today** (Partial): Credit check from documents (open receivable less unapplied cash plus unbilled orders) parks an order on hold at confirm and refuses fulfilment over the limit; release by admin, owner or finance with audit; desk hold banner and release. **Missing:** No credit approval workflow (request, approver, limit change history), no customer-level credit hold or stop flag, no check on refactor/v1 counter sales; delivery completion skips the check by design. **Open work:** PR 72: credit check on the counter's ACCOUNT tender under the customer's credit lock. Evidence: [`core/internal/order/service.go:1381`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/order/service.go#L1381), [`core/internal/order/fulfil.go:713`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/order/fulfil.go#L713), [`core/internal/order/service.go:973`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/order/service.go#L973), [`core/internal/order/wire_test.go:769`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/order/wire_test.go#L769), [`core/internal/order/round2_test.go:176`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/order/round2_test.go#L176), [`web/apps/desk/src/pages/orders/OrderDetail.ts:110`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/web/apps/desk/src/pages/orders/OrderDetail.ts#L110). ## AR-05

**Finance or service charges.** Priority **MUST**: Finance charges are named in the goal and documented at all three.

- **Rundoo** (Yes, documented): Finance charges with percent, compounding, minimum balance, minimum fee and grace period, assessed during the statement run; individual charges can be voided. [R40](https://www.rundocs.com/help/accounts-receivable/finance-charges/)
- **BisTrack** (Yes, behind a demo or login): A Finance Charge Browser on the Accounts Receivable tab generates, reviews, edits and applies finance charges before statements run; an overdue days threshold, per customer grace days and rate override, and a company default rate in system options. [B73](https://ondemand.epicor.com/courseResources/r2061_FinanceChargesTranscript_1.pdf)
- **Agility** (Yes, documented): Finance charges per bill-to with minimum charge and threshold; finance charge invoices tracked in A/R; items placed on hold stop accruing. [A27](https://restdoc.dmsi.com/) [A111](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Accounts_Receivable/a.rcreditdisputeentry.htm)
- **Gable today** (Missing): Nothing computes or bills finance or service charges. **Missing:** Finance charge rules (rate, minimum, grace, exempt customers), a periodic run that bills overdue balances, and statement presentation. (no screen)

## AR-09

**Terms master and early payment discounts.** Priority **MUST**: Built in the API; the terms screen and the counter discount remain.

- **Rundoo** (Yes, documented): Finance terms set by invoice, statement, EOM or statement EOM reference date with net days and optional early payment discount percent and days; no per sale override. [R38](https://www.rundocs.com/help/accounts-receivable/finance-terms/)
- **BisTrack** (Yes, documented): A payment terms master (code, name, description) sets invoice due dates and early payment discounts, and a term is chosen on each customer. On the payables side AP Assist captures supplier terms and early payment discounts. [B75](https://ondemand.epicor.com/courseResources/r2214_CustomerMasterTables_Transcript_1.pdf) [B74](https://ondemand.epicor.com/courseResources/r2046_CustomerAccountsOverviewTranscript_1.pdf) [B14](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/products/enterprise-resource-planning-erp/bistrack/ap-assist/)
- **Agility** (Yes, documented): Payment terms with discount dates and grace days; alternate terms by payment method, for example no discount on card payments.
