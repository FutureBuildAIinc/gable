# Parity rows, SO: Sales orders, special orders, drop ship and will call

The detail behind the [SO rows of the parity matrix](../LBM-PARITY-MATRIX.md#so-sales-orders-special-orders-drop-ship-and-will-call). R, B and A refs link the Rundoo, BisTrack and Agility pages each claim rests on; Gable's evidence links its code on `refactor/v1` at `58dde7e8`.

## SO-01

**Sales orders (lines, ship to, job, customer PO, several ship dates or releases).** Priority **MUST**: Orders with releases and ship dates per line are core.

- **Rundoo** (Yes, documented): Held sales (will-call or special order) carry lines, customer PO, job and ship to address; item sets group lines; partial close outs release and invoice parts as goods arrive, drawing down deposits. [R32](https://www.rundocs.com/help/pos/drafts-quotes-will-calls/) [R48](https://rundoo.substack.com/p/rundoo-primer-partial-closeouts-and) [R24](https://www.rundocs.com/help/pos/sales/) [R85](https://www.rundocs.com/walkthroughs/schedule-deliveries/) [R2](https://rundoo.ai/industries/lumber/)
- **BisTrack** (Yes, marketing only): Order management covers pricing, entry, fulfilment, delivery and invoicing; orders carry multiple jobsite delivery addresses and call off releases; OCR and EDI can create orders from customer POs. [B1](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/products/enterprise-resource-planning-erp/bistrack/) [B21](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/solutions/industries/building-supply/hardscapes/) [B31](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/products/enterprise-resource-planning-erp/bistrack/ptw-order-entry-automation/) [B50](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/solutions/industries/building-supply/epicor-bistrack-for-distributors/)
- **Agility** (Yes, documented): Sales orders with ship-to, job, customer PO, sale type, header and line expected dates, routes, several shipments per order and partial invoicing. Multi yard: Orders carry branch ids; lines can fill from another branch. [A27](https://restdoc.dmsi.com/) [A50](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Industry_Functions/retail.prodealersindustry.htm) [A72](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Data_Files/saletypes.htm)

## SO-02

**Special orders (non stock; a linked PO from the order; the receipt allocated to the order).** Priority **MUST**: Special orders with a linked PO and the receipt allocated to the order are core LBM.

- **Rundoo** (Yes, documented): Special orderable products; the sale line links to a vendor PO through the Buyers list and tracks Created, Ordered, Received; partial receipts; sale forms capture specs; no automatic customer notice on arrival. Multi yard: Special orders tab filters by location or all locations. [R33](https://www.rundocs.com/help/pos/special-orders/)
- **BisTrack** (Yes, marketing only): Special order handling; special orders reach a manager's dashboard as POs for approval and can be combined to meet vendor minimums; salespeople are alerted when special items arrive. - **Agility** (Yes, documented): Non-stock items created at entry from templates or Item Builder with linked POs generated from the order; pass-thru items carry received cost to the order without averaging; stock status and PO check-in lists track both sides. [A64](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Sales_Transactions/non.stockitemprocessing.htm) [A50](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Industry_Functions/retail.prodealersindustry.htm) [A69](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Procedures_Processes/pass.thruitemsandtransactions.htm) [A20](https://www.dmsi.com/resources/non-stop-non-stocks-keep-a-cleaner-catalog-with-agility/) [A27](https://restdoc.dmsi.com/)

## SO-03

**Drop ship (the vendor ships direct to the customer; linked PO).** Priority **MUST**: Drop ship is named in the goal and common at every dealer.

- **Rundoo** (Partial, documented): Drop ship handled by setting the PO ship-to to the customer's address; goods never touch inventory. No separate drop ship order type described. Multi yard: PO ship-to defaults to the signed in location and is switched to the customer's address for a drop ship. [R33](https://www.rundocs.com/help/pos/special-orders/) [R59](https://www.rundocs.com/help/vendors-purchasing/purchase-orders/)
- **BisTrack** (Not public, nothing public): No source describes drop ship orders with a linked PO. B7 only names a direct sales supplier invoice type, and a search found nothing more. [B7](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/products/enterprise-resource-planning-erp/bistrack/bistrack-financial-management-tools/)
- **Agility** (Yes, documented): Direct (drop ship) sale types do not affect inventory and can auto create the linked PO; split POs let one large buy drop ship parts to several customers. [A72](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Data_Files/saletypes.htm) [A99](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Purchase_Orders/splitpurchaseorders.htm)
- **Gable today** (Missing): Only the cost side: a non-stock or direct ship order line relieves inventory value from its linked purchase receipt lines at billing. **Missing:** A drop ship flow: a PO that ships to the customer's address, receipt without stock, the order billed from the vendor's ship confirmation, and a desk screen. Evidence: [`core/internal/order/fulfil.go:767`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/order/fulfil.go#L767), [`core/internal/order/repository.go:1209`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/order/repository.go#L1209).
