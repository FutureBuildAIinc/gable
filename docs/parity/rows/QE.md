# Parity rows, QE: Quotes, estimates and takeoffs

The detail behind the [QE rows of the parity matrix](../LBM-PARITY-MATRIX.md#qe-quotes-estimates-and-takeoffs). R, B and A refs link the Rundoo, BisTrack and Agility pages each claim rests on; Gable's evidence links its code on `refactor/v1` at `58dde7e8`.

## QE-01

**Quotes with expiry and revisions, converted to orders.** Priority **MUST**: Quotes that expire and revise, then convert, are the start of every contractor sale.

- **Rundoo** (Yes, documented): Quotes need a customer, carry an expiration date, print or email, pin line prices at save, can be edited or re-quoted, and convert to a sale. No revision history described. Multi yard: Default quote expiration is a company wide setting. [R32](https://www.rundocs.com/help/pos/drafts-quotes-will-calls/) [R78](https://www.rundocs.com/help/admin-settings/communications/) [R6](https://rundoo.ai/product/pos-web-app/)
- **BisTrack** (Partial, documented): Quotes are their own numbered transaction; staff look up, accept and transfer quotes to orders or call off orders, and quotes can be sent for DocuSign. Quote expiry dates and revision tracking are not described publicly. Add on or partner: Epicor ECM with DocuSign for signed quotes. [B69](https://www.mydoitbest.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Whats-New-in-BisTrack-2023.2.pdf) [B6](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/products/enterprise-resource-planning-erp/bistrack/outside-sales-management-tools/) [B60](https://www.g2.com/products/epicor-bistrack/reviews)
- **Agility** (Yes, documented): Quotes with active days and close dates release fully or partly to one or more orders and can be extended; master, merged and escalator quotes; change orders in project management. Formal revision numbering is not described. Multi yard: Quotes can take the branch from the ship-to default; quote lines carry a shipping branch. [A51](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Sales_Transactions/quoteentry.htm) [A50](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Industry_Functions/retail.prodealersindustry.htm) [A54](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Sales_Transactions/projectmanagement.htm) [A27](https://restdoc.dmsi.com/)
- **Gable today** (Partial): Quotes on the module recipe: numbered, branch-walled, priced lines with unit pairs, expires_at, draft-sent-accepted-rejected-expired lifecycle with events, convert creating the order in one transaction; desk list, builder and detail. **Missing:** Expiry never enforced (no auto-expire; accept and convert ignore expires_at); revision is a concurrency counter, no stored versions; a quote accepted first (desk or portal) can never convert to an order. **The MVP:** backend/internal/quote: PUT /quotes/{id}/state and a convert that returned a payload for the client to post, not an order in one transaction Evidence: [`core/internal/quote/handler.go:52`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/quote/handler.go#L52), [`core/internal/quote/handler.go:60`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/quote/handler.go#L60), [`core/internal/quote/service.go:827`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/quote/service.go#L827), [`core/internal/quote/service.go:882`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/quote/service.go#L882), [`core/internal/quote/service.go:1069`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/quote/service.go#L1069), [`core/internal/quote/wire_test.go:1023`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/quote/wire_test.go#L1023), [`core/internal/quote/convert_branch_test.go:153`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/quote/convert_branch_test.go#L153), [`web/apps/desk/src/pages/quotes/QuoteDetail.ts:501`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/web/apps/desk/src/pages/quotes/QuoteDetail.ts#L501). ## QE-02

**Job estimates (phases, house plans, packages, bid sections).** Priority **SHOULD**: Phased job estimates are documented fully only by Agility.

- **Rundoo** (Partial, documented): Named item sets split one quote or sale into groups (house, garage, deck) with subtotals that print as sections; a large quote sells as several sales with deposits. No house plan, package or bid section objects. No native house plan or bid section estimator is described. Add on or partner: TradeTek (partner); older Epicor Professional Estimating.
