# Parity rows, QE: Quotes, estimates and takeoffs

The detail behind the [QE rows of the parity matrix](../LBM-PARITY-MATRIX.md#qe-quotes-estimates-and-takeoffs). R, B and A refs link the Rundoo, BisTrack and Agility pages each claim rests on; Gable's evidence links its code on `refactor/v1` at `695d587c`.

## QE-01

**Quotes with expiry and revisions, converted to orders.** Priority **MUST**: Quotes that expire and revise, then convert, are the start of every contractor sale.

- **Rundoo** (Yes, documented): Quotes need a customer, carry an expiration date, print or email, pin line prices at save, can be edited or re-quoted, and convert to a sale. No revision history described. Multi yard: Default quote expiration is a company wide setting. [R32](https://www.rundocs.com/help/pos/drafts-quotes-will-calls/) [R78](https://www.rundocs.com/help/admin-settings/communications/) [R6](https://rundoo.ai/product/pos-web-app/)
- **BisTrack** (Partial, documented): Quotes are their own numbered transaction; staff look up, accept and transfer quotes to orders or call off orders, and quotes can be sent for DocuSign. Quote expiry dates and revision tracking are not described publicly. Add on or partner: Epicor ECM with DocuSign for signed quotes. [B69](https://www.mydoitbest.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Whats-New-in-BisTrack-2023.2.pdf) [B6](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/products/enterprise-resource-planning-erp/bistrack/outside-sales-management-tools/) [B60](https://www.g2.com/products/epicor-bistrack/reviews)
- **Agility** (Yes, documented): Quotes with active days and close dates release fully or partly to one or more orders and can be extended; master, merged and escalator quotes; change orders in project management. Formal revision numbering is not described. Multi yard: Quotes can take the branch from the ship-to default; quote lines carry a shipping branch. [A51](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Sales_Transactions/quoteentry.htm) [A50](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Industry_Functions/retail.prodealersindustry.htm) [A54](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Sales_Transactions/projectmanagement.htm) [A27](https://restdoc.dmsi.com/)
- **The MVP** (Partial, master): Quotes with lines, states (draft, sent, accepted, rejected, expired), expires_at stored, edit while draft, a branch; float dollars; convert marks the quote accepted and returns a payload for the client to post. No expiry enforcement, revisions or tests.
- **Gable today** (Partial): Quotes on the module recipe: numbered, branch-walled (including no-header grants), customerlink branch linking, priced lines with unit pairs, expires_at, lifecycle with events, convert of draft, sent, or already-accepted quotes in one transaction, branded quote PDF/email routes, and split-pane QuoteBuilder.ts. **Missing:** Expiry is not enforced automatically or on accept/convert; revision is an optimistic-concurrency counter rather than saved version snapshots; QuoteDetail.ts:623 hides the Convert button when status is accepted (QuoteList.ts:295 shows it) and has no Print/Email PDF button. Evidence: [`core/internal/quote/service.go:833`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/695d587c5d7431194005a93bec51deb53c3e2749/core/internal/quote/service.go#L833), [`core/internal/quote/service.go:893`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/695d587c5d7431194005a93bec51deb53c3e2749/core/internal/quote/service.go#L893), [`core/internal/document/forms.go:22`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/695d587c5d7431194005a93bec51deb53c3e2749/core/internal/document/forms.go#L22), [`web/apps/desk/src/pages/QuoteBuilder.ts:86`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/695d587c5d7431194005a93bec51deb53c3e2749/web/apps/desk/src/pages/QuoteBuilder.ts#L86), [`web/apps/desk/src/pages/quotes/QuoteList.ts:295`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/695d587c5d7431194005a93bec51deb53c3e2749/web/apps/desk/src/pages/quotes/QuoteList.ts#L295), [`web/apps/desk/src/pages/quotes/QuoteDetail.ts:623`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/695d587c5d7431194005a93bec51deb53c3e2749/web/apps/desk/src/pages/quotes/QuoteDetail.ts#L623).
