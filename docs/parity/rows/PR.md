# Parity rows, PR: Pricing

The detail behind the [PR rows of the parity matrix](../LBM-PARITY-MATRIX.md#pr-pricing). R, B and A refs link the Rundoo, BisTrack and Agility pages each claim rests on; Gable's evidence links its code on `refactor/v1` at `58dde7e8`.

## PR-01

**Price matrices (customer class or level by product group; margin, markup or list less).** Priority **MUST**: Price matrices are named in the goal and core at all three.

- **Rundoo** (Yes, documented): Manual or rule-based price tiers; rules scope by vendor, department, class, fineline, tag or product and price by discount off a reference tier, margin on cost or dollar; each customer sits on one tier. Multi yard: Tiers are company wide; pricing regions add per location prices on manual tiers. [R44](https://www.rundocs.com/help/pricing/pricing-tiers/) [R43](https://www.rundocs.com/help/pricing/product-prices/) [R8](https://rundoo.ai/product/manage-pricing-purchasing/) [R48](https://rundoo.substack.com/p/rundoo-primer-partial-closeouts-and)
- **BisTrack** (Yes, marketing only): Pricing rules by customer segment with rule based controls; integrations reuse BisTrack pricing tables for customer and volume discounts; a reviewer calls pricing options robust. Matrix mechanics are not public. [B21](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/solutions/industries/building-supply/hardscapes/) [B48](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/solutions/industries/building-supply/hardwood-lumber/) [B29](https://assets.epicor.com/m/7a1ffd708788d49d/original/BisTrack-API-eBook.pdf) [B60](https://www.g2.com/products/epicor-bistrack/reviews)
- **Agility** (Yes, documented): A 24 step price and discount hierarchy searches customer, ship-to, pricing group and standard records by item, price code and sale type; formula prices by margin or multiplier. - **Gable today** (Partial): Category price matrix: rules by customer tier or account over a category tree with inheritance, markup on cost, margin on cost, markdown off list or fixed, dated, prioritised, audited, bulk edit; resolve preview; desk matrix screen. **Missing:** No branch dimension (a matrix cannot differ by yard; PR-04); off unless CATEGORY_PRICING_ENABLED=true, which the stack never sets; cost basis is one average cost per product; tiers a fixed enum until ADR 0006 levels. **The MVP:** backend/internal/pricing/category_service.go: the same category engine, also flag gated. Evidence: [`core/internal/app/serve/serve.go:383`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/app/serve/serve.go#L383), [`core/internal/pricing/category_handler.go:51`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/pricing/category_handler.go#L51), [`core/internal/pricing/category_model.go:44`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/pricing/category_model.go#L44), [`core/internal/pricing/category_service.go:83`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/pricing/category_service.go#L83), [`core/internal/pricing/service.go:170`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/pricing/service.go#L170), [`core/internal/pricing/category_service_test.go:320`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/pricing/category_service_test.go#L320), [`core/internal/pricing/category_wire_test.go:314`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/pricing/category_wire_test.go#L314), [`web/apps/desk/src/pages/admin/pricing/PricingMatrix.ts:24`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/web/apps/desk/src/pages/admin/pricing/PricingMatrix.ts#L24). ## PR-04

**Branch pricing (prices differ by yard).** Priority **MUST**: Branch pricing is named in the goal and is the multi yard pricing case.

- **Rundoo** (Partial, documented): Locations can be grouped into pricing regions with a price per region on manual tiers; rule-based tiers do not yet support regions. Custom prices and promotions can be limited to locations. Multi yard: Region pricing is the branch price mechanism; enabled under Admin Locations. - **BisTrack** (Yes, documented): Each branch can carry its own selling prices; blank branch prices fall back to company level prices, and selling price rules are checked first. A 31 location dealer changes prices across product lines and branches with SQL routines. Multi yard: Company level prices with per branch overrides; keeping prices at company level only gives uniform pricing. [B81](https://ondemand.epicor.com/courseResources/r2212_PricesTab_Transcript_1.pdf) [B67](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/resources/success-stories/building-supply/tal-building-centers/) [B48](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/solutions/industries/building-supply/hardwood-lumber/) [B8](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/products/enterprise-resource-planning-erp/bistrack/bistrack-api/)

Multi yard: Core multi yard pricing control.
