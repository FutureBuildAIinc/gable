# Parity rows, IN: Inventory across branches

The detail behind the [IN rows of the parity matrix](../LBM-PARITY-MATRIX.md#in-inventory-across-branches). R, B and A refs link the Rundoo, BisTrack and Agility pages each claim rests on; Gable's evidence links its code on `refactor/v1` at `58dde7e8`.

## IN-01

**On hand by branch or yard and by bin or location.** Priority **MUST**: On hand by yard and bin is the base of multi yard stock.

- **Rundoo** (Yes, documented): Per location row for each product with bin, on hand, on order, committed, min and max; bin prints on labels and pick lists. Multi yard: On hand and bin held per location; product list defaults to the current location. [R52](https://www.rundocs.com/help/inventory/products/) [R8](https://rundoo.ai/product/manage-pricing-purchasing/) [R2](https://rundoo.ai/industries/lumber/)
- **BisTrack** (Yes, documented): Real time stock levels across locations; stock on hand by branch and category; bin locations for picking, labels and stock checks. Multi yard: Quantity by location inside a branch needs Epicor WMS Foundations or Enterprise, which is enabled branch by branch. Add on or partner: Epicor WMS for location based item tracking. [B4](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/products/enterprise-resource-planning-erp/bistrack/order-management-tools/) [B7](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/products/enterprise-resource-planning-erp/bistrack/bistrack-financial-management-tools/) [B2](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/products/enterprise-resource-planning-erp/bistrack/ptw-mobile-warehouse-applications/) [B27](https://assets.epicor.com/m/37c597a01d93eb79/original/Epicor-BisTrack-Yard-Warehouse-Management-Options-Flyer.pdf) [B69](https://www.mydoitbest.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Whats-New-in-BisTrack-2023.2.pdf)
- **Agility** (Yes, documented): On hand and available by branch; locations and sublocations with default and permanent locations per item; lot and tag tracking. Multi yard: Branch inventory lists return quantities for every branch. [A84](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Data_Files/locations.htm) [A27](https://restdoc.dmsi.com/)
- **Gable today** (Partial): Stock rows per product and location; locations form a branch tree down to bins; the levels list (quantity, allocated, available) is walled by X-Branch-Id or grants; product totals per caller branch; desk stock tab lists rows by location. **Missing:** Counter sales and voids move a row with no location, so no branch; no unique stock identity (duplicate rows possible); no branch field or filter on a level row; no desk screen to build the bin tree. **Open work:** PR 72: a counter sale takes stock at the register's branch (IssueQty) and a write naming no location picks a row at the caller's branch, not the unassigned row.. **The MVP:** app/src/pages/Locations.ts with components/location/LocationManager.ts: an unrouted zones, aisles and bins manager; refactor/v1 dropped it. Evidence: [`core/internal/inventory/levels.go:81`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/inventory/levels.go#L81), [`core/internal/inventory/repository.go:57`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/inventory/repository.go#L57), [`core/internal/pos/service.go:320`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/pos/service.go#L320), [`core/internal/location/model.go:24`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/location/model.go#L24), [`core/internal/inventory/wire_test.go:132`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/inventory/wire_test.go#L132), [`web/apps/desk/src/pages/inventory/tabs/ProductStockTab.ts:147`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/web/apps/desk/src/pages/inventory/tabs/ProductStockTab.ts#L147). ## IN-02

**Committed or allocated quantities.** Priority **MUST**: Built; allocations by bin or lot follow the stock identity.

- **BisTrack** (Yes, documented): Stock is allocated to order lines (a stock allocation record), and order entry stock warnings can allocate from replacement products; integrators describe availability net of allocations and back orders. ## IN-04

**Transfers between branches (request, ship, in transit, receive).** Priority **MUST**: Transfers between yards are named in the goal.

- **Rundoo** (Yes, documented): Transfers move New, Drafts, Requested, Sent (in transit), Received or Voided; partial receipt leaves the remainder outstanding; Dooey can propose quantities from each location's velocity; valued at average cost. Multi yard: Request by the receiving store and approval by the sending store; owner texted on a request; no per location approval setting. a dealer moves stock between its two yards.
