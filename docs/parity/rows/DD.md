# Parity rows, DD: Delivery and dispatch

The detail behind the [DD rows of the parity matrix](../LBM-PARITY-MATRIX.md#dd-delivery-and-dispatch). R, B and A refs link the Rundoo, BisTrack and Agility pages each claim rests on; Gable's evidence links its code on `refactor/v1` at `695d587c`.

## DD-01

**Delivery scheduling and the dispatch board.** Priority **MUST**: A dispatch board per yard, with each truck and driver at a home yard, is the multi yard delivery case.

- **Rundoo** (Yes, documented): Deliveries tab with calendar (week or month) and list; runs have a departure date, carrier, driver and stops by address; a To schedule queue holds sales staged for delivery. Multi yard: Calendar shows one location at a time; list view filters several locations for a central dispatcher; no shared cross location calendar yet. [R34](https://www.rundocs.com/help/pos/scheduled-deliveries/) [R2](https://rundoo.ai/industries/lumber/)
- **BisTrack** (Yes, documented): Journey Planner handles delivery scheduling, vehicle selection and manifests; staff drag delivery notes onto vehicle journeys, with change order and wrong day alerts; schedules are visible to sales. Multi yard: Dealers run centralised dispatch for several yards. [B3](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/products/enterprise-resource-planning-erp/bistrack/ptw-journey-planner-and-delivery/) [B69](https://www.mydoitbest.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Whats-New-in-BisTrack-2023.2.pdf) [B68](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/resources/success-stories/building-supply/alliance-lumber/) [B20](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/solutions/industries/building-supply/pro-dealers/) [B48](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/solutions/industries/building-supply/hardwood-lumber/)
- **Agility** (Partial, documented): Orders carry expected delivery dates and routes; dispatch entry and the dispatch viewer manage trucks and stops; route capacity shows load per route. A drag and drop dispatch board is not described. [A142](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Dispatch/dispatchprocessing.htm) [A143](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Procedures_Processes/dispatchviewer.htm) [A145](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Sales_Transactions/routecapacity.htm)
- **The MVP** (Partial, master): Route create/list, stop assignment, dispatch, and complete (delivery/handler.go:49, service.go:240,262, service_test.go:107) plus DispatchBoard.ts. No branch_id on routes, vehicles, or drivers (model.go:51-101), no branch wall on reads, no route edit/cancel, and delivery windows are never populated.
- **Gable today** (Partial): Routes per scheduled date with vehicle and driver; orders assigned as stops (pickup orders refused); the board read embeds stops; draft, in transit, completed; a delivered stop queues fulfilment; walled through stops' orders; desk dispatch board. **Missing:** No yard on a route, truck or driver (the fleet is dealer wide), so no per yard board; empty routes show to every branch; no route cancel or edit; delivery windows stored but never written. Evidence: [`core/internal/delivery/handler.go:67`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/695d587c5d7431194005a93bec51deb53c3e2749/core/internal/delivery/handler.go#L67), [`core/internal/delivery/service.go:1020`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/695d587c5d7431194005a93bec51deb53c3e2749/core/internal/delivery/service.go#L1020), [`core/internal/delivery/model.go:220`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/695d587c5d7431194005a93bec51deb53c3e2749/core/internal/delivery/model.go#L220), [`core/internal/delivery/wire_test.go:525`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/695d587c5d7431194005a93bec51deb53c3e2749/core/internal/delivery/wire_test.go#L525), [`web/apps/desk/src/pages/DispatchBoard.ts:15`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/695d587c5d7431194005a93bec51deb53c3e2749/web/apps/desk/src/pages/DispatchBoard.ts#L15). ## DD-02

Add on or partner: Trimble Multi Vehicle Routing (partner) for capacity aware optimisation.
