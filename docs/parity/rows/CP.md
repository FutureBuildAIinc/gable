# Parity rows, CP: Counter and POS

The detail behind the [CP rows of the parity matrix](../LBM-PARITY-MATRIX.md#cp-counter-and-pos). R, B and A refs link the Rundoo, BisTrack and Agility pages each claim rests on; Gable's evidence links its code on `refactor/v1` at `695d587c`.

## CP-01

**Cash sale at the counter (walk in or cash customer, receipt).** Priority **MUST**: Every yard rings cash sales; the sale must move the register's own yard's stock and post in its transaction.

- **Rundoo** (Yes, documented): Counter sale from cart to payment to receipt; walk in cash and card sales complete without a customer; scan, search or browse departments to add lines. Multi yard: Sales ring at the signed in location; staff with permission switch location from the header without signing out. [R24](https://www.rundocs.com/help/pos/sales/) [R6](https://rundoo.ai/product/pos-web-app/) [R20](https://www.rundocs.com/help/admin-settings/locations/) [R21](https://www.rundocs.com/help/admin-settings/permissions/)
- **BisTrack** (Yes, marketing only): Counter sales and point of sale are core functional areas; retail POS offers touchscreen checkout and barcode scanning for walk-in and pro customers; dashboards total cash sales separately from charge sales. Multi yard: Daily cash and charge sale totals appear in dashboards; a per branch split is not described. [B60](https://www.g2.com/products/epicor-bistrack/reviews) [B49](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/solutions/industries/building-supply/home-centers/) [B7](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/products/enterprise-resource-planning-erp/bistrack/bistrack-financial-management-tools/) [B52](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/blog/industries/how-a-cross-functional-erp-can-optimize-your-building-supply-business/)
- **Agility** (Yes, documented): Optional Register POS mode of sales order entry for counter and walk-in sales: generic or named walk-in customers, barcode item entry, payment and invoice in one step, 3 inch receipts, customer line display. Multi yard: POS orders can use the pickup branch's register tax code and post to the branch cash account. DMSi markets its separate Frameworks product as the dealer POS. [A28](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Sales_Transactions/registerpointofsaletransactions.htm) [A50](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Industry_Functions/retail.prodealersindustry.htm) [A33](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Sales_Transactions/linedisplay.htm) [A42](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Data_Files/fielddescriptionsforpaymentmethodmaintenance.htm) [A23](https://www.dmsi.com/resources/kelly-fradet-lumber/) [A3](https://www.dmsi.com/dealers/) [A154](https://www.dmsi.com/resources/beisser-lumber/) [A155](https://www.dmsi.com/independent-erp-partner/)
- **The MVP** (Partial, its staging line, its community line, master): POS API: start, add, remove, complete, void, history, product search; cents totals; stock moved at a default location; GL posted after commit as a cash sale; master leaves tax at zero, staging taxes; no tests on the sale path.
- **Gable today** (Built): Counter sale on the module recipe: defaults to WALK-IN, issues stock at the register's branch, posts a counter invoice with tax and COGS, and applies cash payment in one transaction; branded PDF receipt print/email; per-yard register and open-till gate. **Missing:** Sale lines sell only in the product's stocking unit (409 unit_not_stock_unit); receipt prints as a full-page PDF rather than thermal tape. ## CP-02

**Account (charge) sale at the counter, with the customer PO and job where the account requires them.** Priority **MUST**: Charge sales to contractor accounts are most of a yard's counter volume.

- **Rundoo** (Yes, documented): Charge account tender posts the sale as an invoice on the customer's balance; per customer toggles require PO, job, buyer, signature or notes before checkout; credit utilisation shown. Multi yard: Charge sales post to the selling location's AR control account but land on one customer balance across stores. [R24](https://www.rundocs.com/help/pos/sales/) [R25](https://www.rundocs.com/help/admin-settings/tender-methods/) [R9](https://rundoo.ai/product/improve-customer-satisfaction/) [R35](https://www.rundocs.com/help/customers-jobs/customers/) [R110](https://rundoo.substack.com/p/rundoo-primer-a-client-story-vetter)
- **BisTrack** (Partial, marketing only): Charge sales to accounts are reported apart from cash sales; customer records hold job sites; integrations push orders with PO numbers and job sites. Whether an account can require a PO or job at entry is not public. [B7](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/products/enterprise-resource-planning-erp/bistrack/bistrack-financial-management-tools/) [B8](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/products/enterprise-resource-planning-erp/bistrack/bistrack-api/) [B58](https://www.dckap.com/blog/epicor-bistrack-ecommerce-integration/)
- **Agility** (Yes, documented): Account sales carry customer PO, job and reference; ship-to rules can require and validate a PO or job number by order type and check duplicate POs; an authorized charge list with photos shows who may charge. Multi yard: Branch ship-to records hold branch specific defaults such as terms, tax and sales agent for each servicing branch. ## CP-03

**Tender types and split tender (cash, check, card, on account, store credit or gift card).** Priority **MUST**: Split tender with validated methods is standard at all three.

- **Rundoo** (Yes, documented): Card, cash, check, charge account, gift card, ACH (receivables), write off and support-added custom tenders; split payment across tenders. The docs conflict on charge account in a split: Tender methods says it is left out, Sales shows check plus charge account. No customer credit tender at the sale. Add on or partner: Custom tenders added by Rundoo Support. [R24](https://www.rundocs.com/help/pos/sales/) [R25](https://www.rundocs.com/help/admin-settings/tender-methods/) [R41](https://www.rundocs.com/help/accounts-receivable/customer-credits/)
- **BisTrack** (Partial, documented): Cash sales and integrated cards at the POS; account payments at the counter, by check or ACH; internal gift cards pay for orders; loyalty points can be redeemed. Split tender and store credit are not described. Add on or partner: Epicor Payments or Shift4 for cards. [B7](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/products/enterprise-resource-planning-erp/bistrack/bistrack-financial-management-tools/) [B69](https://www.mydoitbest.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Whats-New-in-BisTrack-2023.2.pdf) [B49](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/solutions/industries/building-supply/home-centers/) [B63](https://www.epicor.com/en/products/digital-commerce/payments)
- **Agility** (Yes, documented): Cash, check, card, ACH, reward credit, open credit (COD) and gift card tenders, plus refunds; up to 99 payments on one transaction, each with its own payment method, so tenders split on one sale. Multi yard: Alternate pay terms by payment method can be set per branch. [A29](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Sales_Transactions/paymentsandrefundsonsalesordersandcreditmemos.htm) [A41](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Data_Files/paymentmethodmaintenance.htm) [A42](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Data_Files/fielddescriptionsforpaymentmethodmaintenance.htm) [A39](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Data_Files/giftcards.htm)

- **Gable today** (Partial): CompleteSale validates the tender enum (cash, check, card, account), turns each cash, check, or card tender into a net payment applied to the sale's counter invoice, leaves account tender open on the invoice, and POSTerminal.ts supports multi-method split tendering. **Missing:** No store credit, gift card, or financing tender; an open credit memo cannot be applied as a tender at the counter register. ## CP-04

**Tender rules (check limits, cash only accounts, PO required, ID, change, over and under tender).** Priority **MUST**: Cash only accounts, PO required and the credit check at the counter protect AR.

- **Rundoo** (Partial, documented): Per customer gates (require PO, job, buyer, receipt signature, notes); register blocks a charge past the credit limit; change calculated on cash; below cost flag. No check limit or ID rules found. Multi yard: Customer checkout requirements sit on the customer record and apply at every location's register. Check limits, ID checks and over or under tender rules not found. Multi yard: Per customer credit settings override the system wide options. ## CP-07

**Deposits and prepayments on orders, applied at invoice.** Priority **MUST**: Built in the API; the counter and the order screen need the deposit flow.

- **Rundoo** (Yes, documented): Deposits on quotes, will-calls and special orders (default required percent, 50% out of the box), several deposits per order, applied at pickup; forfeits book to a forfeited deposits account.
