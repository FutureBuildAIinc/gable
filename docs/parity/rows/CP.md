# Parity rows, CP: Counter and POS

The detail behind the [CP rows of the parity matrix](../LBM-PARITY-MATRIX.md#cp-counter-and-pos). R, B and A refs link the Rundoo, BisTrack and Agility pages each claim rests on; Gable's evidence links its code on `refactor/v1` at `58dde7e8`.

## CP-01

**Cash sale at the counter (walk in or cash customer, receipt).** Priority **MUST**: Every yard rings cash sales; the sale must move the register's own yard's stock and post in its transaction.

- **Rundoo** (Yes, documented): Counter sale from cart to payment to receipt; walk in cash and card sales complete without a customer; scan, search or browse departments to add lines. Multi yard: Sales ring at the signed in location; staff with permission switch location from the header without signing out. [R24](https://www.rundocs.com/help/pos/sales/) [R6](https://rundoo.ai/product/pos-web-app/) [R20](https://www.rundocs.com/help/admin-settings/locations/) [R21](https://www.rundocs.com/help/admin-settings/permissions/)
- **BisTrack** (Yes, marketing only): Counter sales and point of sale are core functional areas; retail POS offers touchscreen checkout and barcode scanning for walk-in and pro customers; dashboards total cash sales separately from charge sales. Multi yard: Daily cash and charge sale totals appear in dashboards; a per branch split is not described. [B60](https://www.g2.com/products/epicor-bistrack/reviews) [B49](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/solutions/industries/building-supply/home-centers/) [B7](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/products/enterprise-resource-planning-erp/bistrack/bistrack-financial-management-tools/) [B52](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/blog/industries/how-a-cross-functional-erp-can-optimize-your-building-supply-business/)
- **Agility** (Yes, documented): Optional Register POS mode of sales order entry for counter and walk-in sales: generic or named walk-in customers, barcode item entry, payment and invoice in one step, 3 inch receipts, customer line display. Multi yard: POS orders can use the pickup branch's register tax code and post to the branch cash account. DMSi markets its separate Frameworks product as the dealer POS. [A28](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Sales_Transactions/registerpointofsaletransactions.htm) [A50](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Industry_Functions/retail.prodealersindustry.htm) [A33](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Sales_Transactions/linedisplay.htm) [A42](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Data_Files/fielddescriptionsforpaymentmethodmaintenance.htm) [A23](https://www.dmsi.com/resources/kelly-fradet-lumber/) [A3](https://www.dmsi.com/dealers/) [A154](https://www.dmsi.com/resources/beisser-lumber/) [A155](https://www.dmsi.com/independent-erp-partner/)
- **Gable today** (Partial): POS sale API: start, add items, complete with cash tender, change from cash only, tax by branch rate or exemption, audit row, void; register stamps branch. Desk POS terminal screen (register hard coded REG-01). **Missing:** Stock moves on the unassigned location row, not the register's yard; GL posts after commit, best effort, tax credited to revenue, no COGS; no receipt print or email; not on the module recipe. **Open work:** PR 72: the sale becomes an invoice with tax and COGS in one transaction, walk-in customer, stock taken at the register's branch. **The MVP:** backend/internal/pos/service.go: the same POS core, without till sessions, Z reports or returns. Evidence: [`core/internal/pos/handler.go:42`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/pos/handler.go#L42), [`core/internal/pos/service.go:205`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/pos/service.go#L205), [`core/internal/pos/service.go:320`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/pos/service.go#L320), [`core/internal/pos/service.go:383`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/pos/service.go#L383), [`core/internal/gl/service.go:536`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/gl/service.go#L536), [`core/internal/pos/register_branch_test.go:58`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/pos/register_branch_test.go#L58), [`web/apps/desk/src/pages/pos/POSTerminal.ts:10`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/web/apps/desk/src/pages/pos/POSTerminal.ts#L10). ## CP-02

**Account (charge) sale at the counter, with the customer PO and job where the account requires them.** Priority **MUST**: Charge sales to contractor accounts are most of a yard's counter volume.

- **Rundoo** (Yes, documented): Charge account tender posts the sale as an invoice on the customer's balance; per customer toggles require PO, job, buyer, signature or notes before checkout; credit utilisation shown. Multi yard: Charge sales post to the selling location's AR control account but land on one customer balance across stores. [R24](https://www.rundocs.com/help/pos/sales/) [R25](https://www.rundocs.com/help/admin-settings/tender-methods/) [R9](https://rundoo.ai/product/improve-customer-satisfaction/) [R35](https://www.rundocs.com/help/customers-jobs/customers/) [R110](https://rundoo.substack.com/p/rundoo-primer-a-client-story-vetter)
- **BisTrack** (Partial, marketing only): Charge sales to accounts are reported apart from cash sales; customer records hold job sites; integrations push orders with PO numbers and job sites. Whether an account can require a PO or job at entry is not public. [B7](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/products/enterprise-resource-planning-erp/bistrack/bistrack-financial-management-tools/) [B8](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/products/enterprise-resource-planning-erp/bistrack/bistrack-api/) [B58](https://www.dckap.com/blog/epicor-bistrack-ecommerce-integration/)
- **Agility** (Yes, documented): Account sales carry customer PO, job and reference; ship-to rules can require and validate a PO or job number by order type and check duplicate POs; an authorized charge list with photos shows who may charge. Multi yard: Branch ship-to records hold branch specific defaults such as terms, tax and sales agent for each servicing branch. [A27](https://restdoc.dmsi.com/) [A50](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Industry_Functions/retail.prodealersindustry.htm) [A92](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Data_Files/customership.tobranchmaintenance.htm)
- **Gable today** (Partial): ACCOUNT tender needs a customer and becomes an AR invoice after commit (best effort). A will-call order (pickup) carries customer PO, job, PO-required and contact checks, credit hold, and bills at fulfilment. **Missing:** POS sale has no customer PO, job, PO-required or credit check; split account tender invoices a fake summary line; desk POS cannot pick a customer, so ACCOUNT fails there. **Open work:** PR 72: ACCOUNT tender refused for the walk-in customer and held to the credit limit under a lock; still no PO or job on a counter sale. Evidence: [`core/internal/pos/service.go:232`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/pos/service.go#L232), [`core/internal/pos/service.go:387`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/pos/service.go#L387), [`core/internal/pos/service.go:441`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/pos/service.go#L441), [`core/internal/order/service.go:1344`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/order/service.go#L1344), [`core/internal/order/service.go:1381`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/order/service.go#L1381), [`core/internal/order/fulfil.go:461`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/order/fulfil.go#L461). ## CP-03

**Tender types and split tender (cash, check, card, on account, store credit or gift card).** Priority **MUST**: Split tender with validated methods is standard at all three.

- **Rundoo** (Yes, documented): Card, cash, check, charge account, gift card, ACH (receivables), write off and support-added custom tenders; split payment across tenders. The docs conflict on charge account in a split: Tender methods says it is left out, Sales shows check plus charge account. No customer credit tender at the sale. Add on or partner: Custom tenders added by Rundoo Support. [R24](https://www.rundocs.com/help/pos/sales/) [R25](https://www.rundocs.com/help/admin-settings/tender-methods/) [R41](https://www.rundocs.com/help/accounts-receivable/customer-credits/)
- **BisTrack** (Partial, documented): Cash sales and integrated cards at the POS; account payments at the counter, by check or ACH; internal gift cards pay for orders; loyalty points can be redeemed. Split tender and store credit are not described. Add on or partner: Epicor Payments or Shift4 for cards. [B7](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/products/enterprise-resource-planning-erp/bistrack/bistrack-financial-management-tools/) [B69](https://www.mydoitbest.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Whats-New-in-BisTrack-2023.2.pdf) [B49](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/solutions/industries/building-supply/home-centers/) [B63](https://www.epicor.com/en/products/digital-commerce/payments)
- **Agility** (Yes, documented): Cash, check, card, ACH, reward credit, open credit (COD) and gift card tenders, plus refunds; up to 99 payments on one transaction, each with its own payment method, so tenders split on one sale. Multi yard: Alternate pay terms by payment method can be set per branch. [A29](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Sales_Transactions/paymentsandrefundsonsalesordersandcreditmemos.htm) [A41](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Data_Files/paymentmethodmaintenance.htm) [A42](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Data_Files/fielddescriptionsforpaymentmethodmaintenance.htm) [A39](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Data_Files/giftcards.htm)

Desk POS has one tender button per method. **Missing:** No store credit, gift card or financing tender; tender method is an unvalidated string; desk takes only one tender per sale, so no split tender on screen. **Open work:** PR 72: tender enum cash, check, card, account validated; each tender becomes a payment applied to the sale's invoice. Evidence: [`core/internal/pos/model.go:65`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/pos/model.go#L65), [`core/internal/pos/service.go:220`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/pos/service.go#L220), [`core/internal/pos/handler.go:447`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/pos/handler.go#L447), [`web/apps/desk/src/pages/pos/POSTerminal.ts:545`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/web/apps/desk/src/pages/pos/POSTerminal.ts#L545), [`web/apps/desk/src/pages/pos/POSTerminal.ts:248`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/web/apps/desk/src/pages/pos/POSTerminal.ts#L248). ## CP-04

**Tender rules (check limits, cash only accounts, PO required, ID, change, over and under tender).** Priority **MUST**: Cash only accounts, PO required and the credit check at the counter protect AR.

- **Rundoo** (Partial, documented): Per customer gates (require PO, job, buyer, receipt signature, notes); register blocks a charge past the credit limit; change calculated on cash; below cost flag. No check limit or ID rules found. Multi yard: Customer checkout requirements sit on the customer record and apply at every location's register. [R24](https://www.rundocs.com/help/pos/sales/) [R32](https://www.rundocs.com/help/pos/drafts-quotes-will-calls/) [R23](https://www.rundocs.com/help/admin-settings/pos-security/) [R35](https://www.rundocs.com/help/customers-jobs/customers/)
- **BisTrack** (Partial, behind a demo or login): A per customer Reference required setting can demand a PO number, warn on duplicates or restrict to listed POs with a cap per PO; cash account customers; orders over the credit limit hold unless released by code or converted to a cash sale. Check limits, ID checks and over or under tender rules not found. Multi yard: Per customer credit settings override the system wide options. [B78](https://ondemand.epicor.com/courseResources/r2206_OptionsandNotesTab_Transcript_1.pdf) [B79](https://ondemand.epicor.com/courseResources/r2047_AddingNewCustomerAccountsTranscript_1.pdf) [B80](https://ondemand.epicor.com/courseResources/r2049_BT122CreditControlsOverviewTranscript_1.pdf)
- **Agility** (Yes, documented): Payment methods can require check, driver's license or authorization numbers and treat overpayment as change, account credit or deposit; full payment required and allowed tenders per customer; IRS 8300 prompts on large cash. Check limits not described. Multi yard: Full payment required is a ship-to or branch ship-to setting. [A42](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Data_Files/fielddescriptionsforpaymentmethodmaintenance.htm) [A41](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Data_Files/paymentmethodmaintenance.htm) [A28](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Sales_Transactions/registerpointofsaletransactions.htm) [A27](https://restdoc.dmsi.com/) [A44](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Accounts_Receivable/irs8300cashpaymenttracking.htm) [A50](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Industry_Functions/retail.prodealersindustry.htm)

Order confirm enforces PO required, contact authority and credit limit. **Missing:** No check limits, cash-only account rule, ID capture, PO-required or credit check at the counter; tender method strings are not validated (any method accepted). **Open work:** PR 72: walk-in ACCOUNT refusal and credit limit check at the counter. Evidence: [`core/internal/pos/service.go:229`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/pos/service.go#L229), [`core/internal/pos/service.go:232`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/pos/service.go#L232), [`core/internal/pos/service.go:245`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/pos/service.go#L245), [`core/internal/pos/service.go:261`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/pos/service.go#L261), [`core/internal/order/service.go:1344`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/order/service.go#L1344), [`core/internal/order/service.go:1358`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/order/service.go#L1358). ## CP-05

**Integrated card processing at the counter (card present terminal, EMV, tap).** Priority **MUST**: All three integrate card present terminals; the live processor account is the dealer's, so the build is the terminal seam and a test terminal.

- **Rundoo** (Yes, documented): Stripe card readers: tap, insert, swipe or manual entry; Rundoo supplies and configures the reader; Stripe is the only processor. Multi yard: Each Stripe reader is paired and assigned to a location. Add on or partner: Stripe (required processor). Multi yard: Gateway choice is set per system and location. Add on or partner: Epicor Credit Card Processing is an optional module; Epicor Payment Application (also in the cloud); Shift4 (partner). [B69](https://www.mydoitbest.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Whats-New-in-BisTrack-2023.2.pdf) [B63](https://www.epicor.com/en/products/digital-commerce/payments) [B32](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/blog/industries/bistrack-2024-2-empowering-your-business-with-enhanced-functionality/) [B64](https://shift4.com/news/shift4-and-epicor-software-provide-emv-to-lumber-and-building-materials-dealers-and-distributors) [B51](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/products/digital-commerce/payments/payment-exchange/)

[A34](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Integrated_Solutions/creditcardinterface.htm) [A35](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/System_Administration/creditcardcloudlanemanagement.htm) [A15](https://www.dmsi.com/partners/)

## CP-06

**Returns and exchanges at the counter (to the original tender, restocking fees, reason codes).** Priority **MUST**: Returns against the original sale, to the original tender, are daily counter work.

## CP-07

**Deposits and prepayments on orders, applied at invoice.** Priority **MUST**: Built in the API; the counter and the order screen need the deposit flow.

- **Rundoo** (Yes, documented): Deposits on quotes, will-calls and special orders (default required percent, 50% out of the box), several deposits per order, applied at pickup; forfeits book to a forfeited deposits account. Multi yard: Forfeited deposits control account mapped per location. [R32](https://www.rundocs.com/help/pos/drafts-quotes-will-calls/) [R33](https://www.rundocs.com/help/pos/special-orders/)
- **BisTrack** (Partial, marketing only): Epicor Payment Exchange processes prepayments, orders pushed through the API carry payment details, and BisTrack Rentals takes configurable deposits. How deposits apply at invoice is not described. Add on or partner: BisTrack Rentals for rental deposits. [B63](https://www.epicor.com/en/products/digital-commerce/payments) [B8](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/products/enterprise-resource-planning-erp/bistrack/bistrack-api/) [B12](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/products/enterprise-resource-planning-erp/bistrack/ptw-bistrack-rentals/)
- **Agility** (Yes, documented): Deposits taken on open orders or on account and applied later; posted to A/R trade or held as a deposit liability until invoicing; pending card or ACH payments process at invoicing.
