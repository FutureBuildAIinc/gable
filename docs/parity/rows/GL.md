# Parity rows, GL: AP, GL and branch consolidated reporting

The detail behind the [GL rows of the parity matrix](../LBM-PARITY-MATRIX.md#gl-ap-gl-and-branch-consolidated-reporting). R, B and A refs link the Rundoo, BisTrack and Agility pages each claim rests on; Gable's evidence links its code on `refactor/v1` at `695d587c`.

## GL-01

**AP (vendor bills, approvals, payments, payment runs, 1099).** Priority **MUST**: AP basics: bills from receipts and payment runs.

- **Rundoo** (Partial, documented): Vouchered POs are the AP queue; bills are marked paid one at a time by printed check, ACH from Rundoo Banking or another method. No payment runs or 1099 found; approvals can run in Bill.com through QuickBooks. Multi yard: Vouchered bills show the receiving location; each vendor payment is booked against a chosen location. Add on or partner: Bill.com through QuickBooks (optional). [R65](https://www.rundocs.com/help/accounting/bills-payments/) [R61](https://www.rundocs.com/help/vendors-purchasing/vouchers/) [R67](https://www.rundocs.com/help/integrations/banking/) [R70](https://www.rundocs.com/help/integrations/quickbooks/) [R62](https://www.rundocs.com/help/vendors-purchasing/vendor-invoices/)
- **BisTrack** (Partial, marketing only): AP is built in: three way matching, holds and approval workflows, overhead, direct sales and third party invoice types; OCR capture through add-ons lets dealers run AP with one person. Vendor payments, payment runs and 1099 are not described, and one reviewer's approved invoices flow on to Dynamics GP. Add on or partner: AP Assist (partner); Epicor AP automation. [B7](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/products/enterprise-resource-planning-erp/bistrack/bistrack-financial-management-tools/) [B50](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/solutions/industries/building-supply/epicor-bistrack-for-distributors/) [B14](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/products/enterprise-resource-planning-erp/bistrack/ap-assist/) [B44](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/resources/success-stories/building-supply/dartmouth-building-supply/) [B71](https://www.softwareadvice.com/scm/epicor-bistrack-profile/)
- **Agility** (Yes, documented): A/P invoice entry and recurring invoices, payment approval, check printing, ACH, virtual and card payments, positive pay and 1099 inquiry. [A105](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Accounts_Payable.html) [A104](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Accounts_Payable/a.ppaymentapproval.htm) [A27](https://restdoc.dmsi.com/)
