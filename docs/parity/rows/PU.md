# Parity rows, PU: Purchasing and receiving

The detail behind the [PU rows of the parity matrix](../LBM-PARITY-MATRIX.md#pu-purchasing-and-receiving). R, B and A refs link the Rundoo, BisTrack and Agility pages each claim rests on; Gable's evidence links its code on `refactor/v1` at `58dde7e8`.

## PU-01

**Purchase orders (stock, special order, drop ship), by branch and central buying.** Priority **MUST**: Purchase orders by branch, with numbers and revisions, are core.

- **Rundoo** (Yes, documented): POs per vendor through Drafts, Ordered, Received, Vouchered, Paid; sent by EDI, email or marked ordered; ship to any location or a custom address; special order lines feed POs from the Buyers list. Multi yard: Ship to defaults to the signed in location and can be switched to buy for another store; no separate central buying or distribution center flow described. [R59](https://www.rundocs.com/help/vendors-purchasing/purchase-orders/) [R33](https://www.rundocs.com/help/pos/special-orders/) [R8](https://rundoo.ai/product/manage-pricing-purchasing/)
- **BisTrack** (Yes, marketing only): Purchase orders raised by buyers, from the bin and from special orders, which can be combined for vendor minimums; the API creates POs. A two yard dealer consolidated all purchasing to one buyer. Multi yard: Central buyers see company-wide stock and can transfer instead of buying. [B2](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/products/enterprise-resource-planning-erp/bistrack/ptw-mobile-warehouse-applications/) [B25](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/resources/success-stories/building-supply/sears-trostel-lumber-and-millwork/) [B66](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/resources/success-stories/building-supply/hamlin-building/) [B38](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/resources/success-stories/building-supply/alexander-lumber-company/) [B29](https://assets.epicor.com/m/7a1ffd708788d49d/original/BisTrack-API-eBook.pdf)
- **Agility** (Yes, documented): POs by branch and purchase type; centralized purchasing for multi location operations; split POs spawn customer POs from one buy; requesting POs for transfers; suggested POs from branch activity. Multi yard: Central buying alongside per branch suggested orders. Add on or partner: Branch Transfers module for requesting POs (licensed add on). [A99](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Purchase_Orders/splitpurchaseorders.htm) [A1](https://www.dmsi.com/products/agility-erp/) [A66](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Procedures_Processes/branchtransfers.htm) [A50](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Industry_Functions/retail.prodealersindustry.htm) [A27](https://restdoc.dmsi.com/) [A125](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/System_Administration/agilitylicenses.htm)
- **Gable today** (Partial): Purchase orders with lines, vendor, status (draft, sent, partial, received) and source (manual, reorder, special order, A2A); branch_id from the caller's context or the default branch; reads walled; desk list, new and detail screens. **Missing:** Drop ship (no customer ship to); central buying across yards; PO numbers, revisions and the money and quantity contract; submit sends from any status; float costs; no branch choice on create. **Open work:** C4-1a WIP: PO numbers, revisions, the unit line shape and the list wall (migration 100). ## PU-02
