# Parity rows, UM: Units of measure and conversions

The detail behind the [UM rows of the parity matrix](../LBM-PARITY-MATRIX.md#um-units-of-measure-and-conversions). R, B and A refs link the Rundoo, BisTrack and Agility pages each claim rests on; Gable's evidence links its code on `refactor/v1` at `58dde7e8`.

## UM-01

**Several units per item (stocking, selling, purchasing) with conversions.** Priority **MUST**: Selling, buying and stocking in different units is core LBM.

- **Rundoo** (Yes, documented): Company unit list; per product conversion factors to a base unit; default units for stocking, pricing, sale, order and transfer; costs and alternate prices by unit; switch units mid sale. Multi yard: A default transfer unit governs stock moved between yards. [R51](https://rundoo.substack.com/p/rundoo-primer-units-of-measure-are) [R3](https://rundoo.ai/industries/lumber/units-of-measure/) [R86](https://www.rundocs.com/help/admin-settings/units-of-measure/)
- **BisTrack** (Yes, marketing only): Buy, stock and sell in different units with conversions for reporting and invoicing (board foot, bundle, pack; tons, pallets, square feet); OCR order capture reads the unit per line. [B48](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/solutions/industries/building-supply/hardwood-lumber/) [B21](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/solutions/industries/building-supply/hardscapes/) [B31](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/products/enterprise-resource-planning-erp/bistrack/ptw-order-entry-automation/)
- **Agility** (Yes, documented): Every item has stocking and costing UOMs plus conversions to other UOMs for entry, invoicing and counts; several conversions can exist between the same UOMs. [A80](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Data_Files/unitofmeasuresettings.htm) [A27](https://restdoc.dmsi.com/)
- **Gable today** (Partial): Dealer unit catalogue (23 seeded codes, six dimensions, CRUD); per-product unit set with stock, sale, price and purchase defaults and exact conversion pairs; quotes sell in any sell unit of the set; inexact quantities refused. **Missing:** Orders, invoices and the counter sell only in the stocking unit (409 unit_not_stock_unit until C3-2B); product price unit held to the stocking unit; PO lines carry no unit; no unit set screen. **Open work:** C4-1a WIP: PO lines gain uom, price unit and pair, still held to the stocking unit with pair 1 and 1. Evidence: [`core/internal/unit/handler.go:26`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/unit/handler.go#L26), [`core/migrations/099_units_catalogue_and_sets.sql:71`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/migrations/099_units_catalogue_and_sets.sql#L71), [`core/internal/product/handler.go:49`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/product/handler.go#L49), [`core/internal/units/convert.go:117`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/units/convert.go#L117), [`core/internal/units/set_test.go:64`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/units/set_test.go#L64), [`core/internal/quote/units_wire_test.go:109`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/quote/units_wire_test.go#L109), [`core/internal/order/service.go:368`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/order/service.go#L368), [`core/internal/product/unitset.go:334`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/product/unitset.go#L334). ## UM-02

**Piece and lineal foot conversions (dimensional lumber by length).** Priority **MUST**: Lineal foot against pieces is core for dimensional lumber.

- **Rundoo** (Yes, documented): EA, PC and LF units per product with conversion factors back to the base unit; decimal quantities switch on per product; feet and inches entry on tally products. [R86](https://www.rundocs.com/help/admin-settings/units-of-measure/) [R3](https://rundoo.ai/industries/lumber/units-of-measure/) [R49](https://rundoo.substack.com/p/rounding-is-now-in-your-hands)
- **BisTrack** (Partial, marketing only): Lineal foot items such as glulam and engineered wood are reported by length; lumber SKUs carry species, grade and dimension, and items carry tally methods. No source shows piece to lineal foot conversion. [B68](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/resources/success-stories/building-supply/alliance-lumber/) [B48](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/solutions/industries/building-supply/hardwood-lumber/) [B4](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/products/enterprise-resource-planning-erp/bistrack/order-management-tools/)
- **Agility** (Yes, documented): Random length lumber items keep one code per thickness and width with dimension records per length; stocking to piece formulas convert pieces to BF or LF. [A81](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Data_Files/randomlengthlumberitemrecords.htm) [A27](https://restdoc.dmsi.com/)
- **Gable today** (Partial): LF is a catalogue unit; a fixed length board stores its LF pair (a 2x4x8 in PCS holds LF 8 to 1) and quote lines convert piece and lineal quantities exactly into the stocking unit. **Missing:** Orders, invoices, counter and receipts cannot sell or receive in LF against a PCS stocking unit (or the reverse); board length is set only at product create; no desk entry beyond typing a unit code on a quote line. **Open work:** C4-1a WIP: PO lines gain uom, price unit and pair, still held to the stocking unit with pair 1 and 1. Evidence: [`core/internal/units/convert.go:117`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/units/convert.go#L117), [`core/internal/quote/units_wire_test.go:109`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/quote/units_wire_test.go#L109), [`core/internal/order/service.go:368`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/order/service.go#L368), [`core/internal/quote/wire_test.go:1104`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/quote/wire_test.go#L1104). (API only, no desk screen)

## UM-03

**Board foot and per thousand (MBF) pricing.** Priority **MUST**: MBF pricing is core for lumber.

- **Rundoo** (Yes, documented): Board foot and MBF are ordinary units; pricing and sale units are separate defaults, so a product priced per MBF rings per piece; tiers follow the conversion or can be unlinked. [R3](https://rundoo.ai/industries/lumber/units-of-measure/) [R86](https://www.rundocs.com/help/admin-settings/units-of-measure/) [R87](https://rundoo.substack.com/p/rundoo-primer-smarter-pricing-real) [R51](https://rundoo.substack.com/p/rundoo-primer-units-of-measure-are)
- **BisTrack** (Partial, marketing only): Hardwood and timber can be bought, stocked and sold by the board foot with conversions; pricing per thousand board feet is not named. [B48](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/solutions/industries/building-supply/hardwood-lumber/)
- **Agility** (Yes, documented): BF and MBF stocking, costing and price UOMs; per BF prices; MBF as an alternate UOM. [A27](https://restdoc.dmsi.com/) [A2](https://www.dmsi.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Agility-Core.pdf) [A1](https://www.dmsi.com/products/agility-erp/)
- **Gable today** (Partial): BF and MBF units; board thickness, width and length derive BF and MBF pairs; quote and order lines may price per MBF with an exact pair and scale 4 price; quote board feet tested. **Missing:** The engine and base price work per stocking unit only; a product's default price unit cannot be MBF unless it stocks in MBF (held until C3-2B); order lines take the client's pair unresolved; no MBF price list. Evidence: [`core/internal/units/tally.go:56`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/units/tally.go#L56), [`core/internal/units/pair.go:108`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/units/pair.go#L108), [`core/internal/quote/units_wire_test.go:146`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/quote/units_wire_test.go#L146), [`core/internal/product/unitset.go:334`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/product/unitset.go#L334), [`core/internal/order/service.go:384`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/order/service.go#L384). (API only, no desk screen)

## UM-04

**Tally (mixed lengths on one line, at sale and at receipt).** Priority **MUST**: Tally is named in the goal.

- **Rundoo** (Partial, documented): Tally Products (newly released): pieces by length, length plus width, or area on one sale line with a printed breakdown and feet and inches entry. Tally at receiving and stock by length not described. [R50](https://rundoo.substack.com/p/tally-products-are-here) [R49](https://rundoo.substack.com/p/rounding-is-now-in-your-hands) [R3](https://rundoo.ai/industries/lumber/units-of-measure/)
- **BisTrack** (Yes, marketing only): Tally methods supported on items; BisTrack Receipt records tallies at receiving; Epicor WMS adds a tally calculator; TradeTek quotes carry tallies. Add on or partner: Epicor WMS tally calculator. [B4](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/products/enterprise-resource-planning-erp/bistrack/order-management-tools/) [B2](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/products/enterprise-resource-planning-erp/bistrack/ptw-mobile-warehouse-applications/) [B18](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/products/enterprise-resource-planning-erp/warehouse-management-wms/) [B15](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/products/enterprise-resource-planning-erp/bistrack/tradetek/)
- **Agility** (Yes, documented): Tallies on orders, receipts and credit memos at lot, tag or content level; tally calculator items store mixed lengths as footage; end, block and chain tally capture. Multi yard: Tally lookup by branch, location, lot or tag. Add on or partner: TallyExpress (separate DMSi AI tally app). [A27](https://restdoc.dmsi.com/) [A83](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Data_Files/tallycalculatoritems.htm) [A1](https://www.dmsi.com/products/agility-erp/) [A14](https://www.dmsi.com/do-you-know-your-numbers/)
- **Gable today** (Partial): Quote lines on random length products carry a tally (pieces by length), deriving linear and board feet exactly; stored in line_tally_rows; tested. **Missing:** No tally on orders, invoices, fulfilment, counter or purchase receipts (the table keys on quote lines only); stock by length deferred to cycle 4; the tally is lost at convert; no tally entry screen. Evidence: [`core/internal/quote/input.go:63`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/quote/input.go#L63), [`core/internal/units/tally.go:31`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/units/tally.go#L31), [`core/migrations/099_units_catalogue_and_sets.sql:457`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/migrations/099_units_catalogue_and_sets.sql#L457), [`core/internal/quote/units_wire_test.go:331`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/quote/units_wire_test.go#L331), [`core/internal/units/tally_test.go:15`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/units/tally_test.go#L15). ## UM-05

**Bundle and unit (pieces per bundle or lift).** Priority **MUST**: Bundle and unit are named in the goal.

- **Rundoo** (Yes, documented): Unit, pallet, box or bundle style units convert to the base unit; order by the unit, stock and sell by the piece; case quantity rounds POs; unit prices can be unlinked from the strict multiple. [R86](https://www.rundocs.com/help/admin-settings/units-of-measure/) [R3](https://rundoo.ai/industries/lumber/units-of-measure/) [R87](https://rundoo.substack.com/p/rundoo-primer-smarter-pricing-real) [R52](https://www.rundocs.com/help/inventory/products/)
- **BisTrack** (Yes, marketing only): Inner and outer packaging quantities and barcodes at receipt; conversions across bundles and packs; Epicor WMS tracks each pallet or bundle by license plate. Add on or partner: Epicor WMS for license plates. [B2](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/products/enterprise-resource-planning-erp/bistrack/ptw-mobile-warehouse-applications/) [B48](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/solutions/industries/building-supply/hardwood-lumber/) [B18](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/products/enterprise-resource-planning-erp/warehouse-management-wms/)
- **Agility** (Yes, documented): UOM conversions for bundles, squares, cartons and pallets; UNIT orders with piece counts; tagged units carry their tally. [A50](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Industry_Functions/retail.prodealersindustry.htm) [A27](https://restdoc.dmsi.com/)
- **Gable today** (Partial): BUNDLE (and BOX, CTN, BAG, SET, RL) are per-product count units whose size each product's unit set states as a pair, so pieces per bundle can be recorded and sold by on quotes. **Missing:** Orders, counter and receipts cannot use the bundle unit (stocking unit only); no lift or unit identity (IN-12) or bundle-level pricing. **Open work:** C4-1a WIP: PO lines gain uom, price unit and pair, still held to the stocking unit with pair 1 and 1. Evidence: [`core/migrations/099_units_catalogue_and_sets.sql:81`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/migrations/099_units_catalogue_and_sets.sql#L81), [`core/internal/units/set.go:53`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/units/set.go#L53), [`core/internal/order/service.go:368`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/order/service.go#L368). (API only, no desk screen)

## UM-06

**Area, sheets, coverage and weight.** Priority **SHOULD**: Coverage rules help; fixed pairs exist.

- **Rundoo** (Partial, documented): Area tally (pieces by length and width to square feet) and any company unit such as square feet or tons; coverage calculators and weight on items not described. [R3](https://rundoo.ai/industries/lumber/units-of-measure/) [R50](https://rundoo.substack.com/p/tally-products-are-here) [R86](https://www.rundocs.com/help/admin-settings/units-of-measure/) [R5](https://rundoo.ai/product/)
- **BisTrack** (Yes, marketing only): Weight, volume and area units with conversions for bulk materials, scale integration for weighed sales, and load weight and volume on delivery journeys. Sheet and coverage calculations are not described. [B21](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/solutions/industries/building-supply/hardscapes/) [B3](https://www.epicor.com/en-us/products/enterprise-resource-planning-erp/bistrack/ptw-journey-planner-and-delivery/)
- **Agility** (Yes, documented): Sheet good items by width and length (plywood, OSB, drywall); weight and load per item; drywall quoting by the thousand square or linear foot. [A82](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Data_Files/sheetgooditems.htm) [A50](https://documentation.dmsi.com/Agility/prod/Industry_Functions/retail.prodealersindustry.htm) [A1](https://www.dmsi.com/products/agility-erp/)
- **Gable today** (Partial): Area (SF, SQ), volume (CF, CY, GAL) and weight (LBS, CWT, TON) units with standard sizes; per-product pairs with an inexact sell row warning (paver SF on PCS); product weight_lbs. **Missing:** No sheets or coverage rules (bags per area, sheets per square) beyond fixed pairs; orders and counter sell only in the stocking unit; no unit set screen. Evidence: [`core/migrations/099_units_catalogue_and_sets.sql:84`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/migrations/099_units_catalogue_and_sets.sql#L84), [`core/internal/units/set_test.go:296`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/units/set_test.go#L296), [`core/internal/product/model.go:146`](https://github.com/FutureBuildAIinc/gable/blob/58dde7e851596e735cab81b08f576b950323f0cc/core/internal/product/model.go#L146).
